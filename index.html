Ece Çağlayan, Ph.D.

Health Informatics & Computational Neuroscience · Middle East Technical University, Ankara

I study how brain networks change in neurodegenerative and neuropsychiatric disorders, using EEG, MEG and fNIRS functional connectivity together with graph theory and machine learning. My background in computer engineering shapes how I work: I build reproducible analysis pipelines and use them to ask clinical questions about Alzheimer's disease, schizophrenia and gamma entrainment.

Research

Gamma entrainment (GENUS)

PI of a METU BAP project combining 40 Hz visual stimulation with a Go/No-Go task. EEG is collected from healthy young adults, healthy older adults and Alzheimer's patients.

MEG in Alzheimer's disease

Source-level MEG analysis of oscillations and multiscale scaling exponents related to cognitive decline (MANTA study, Amsterdam UMC).

Connectivity in schizophrenia

PhD work on N-Back functional connectivity across M1 receptor polymorphism groups, using PCC, WTC, MI, graph metrics and NBS-predict.
